## Are you the author?

- **Want credit?** Open an issue using the "Credit or removal request" template,
  or email tayyabsabir72@gmail.com. We will add your name and link to `data/meta.json`.
- **Want it removed?** Same process. Valid requests are handled quickly:
  the image is hidden from the site and removed from the repository.

Wallpapers are meant for personal use as desktop and phone backgrounds.
If you want to reuse or redistribute one, check the credited source and its license first.
